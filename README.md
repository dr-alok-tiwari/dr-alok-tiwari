## About

I work at the intersection of medical AI and management education — which is less common a combination than it sounds. My PhD research focused on weakly supervised segmentation in cardiac MRI and transfer learning for COVID-19 detection. At GIM Goa, I teach healthcare analytics, MLOps, and machine learning to PGDM students who mostly aren't coders, which means the challenge is translation, not just instruction.

Outside the classroom, I'm usually somewhere in a manuscript pipeline: computational psychiatry, agricultural ML, responsible AI disclosure, fairness-aware credit scoring. Several are under review; a couple more are nearly there.

If you're working on medical imaging AI, explainable AI, or AI in health systems, I'm likely interested in talking.
